# Food-system domain taxonomy

The notebook applies a rule-based text-mining approach to assign each AI-food publication to a primary food-system domain based on matches in title, abstract, author keywords, and index keywords.

Primary domains used in the manuscript include:

- Nutrition, diet, and health
- Agricultural production
- Food safety, quality, and authentication
- Food security and access
- Sustainability and environment
- Supply chain and resilience
- Processing, preservation, and packaging
- Food waste and loss
- Alternative protein
- General food AI

The full keyword logic is implemented in the notebook and should be reported in supplementary information when submitting the manuscript.
